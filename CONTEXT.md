# Dreams domain glossary

## Task

A content request supplied by a Google Sheet row. Eligible work is marked Not Posted and selected in sheet order.

## Job

One execution of an accepted task through content creation and publication. Its recorded progress includes what completed and what failed.

## Completed

A job whose video has been successfully published to YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook with verified links for all three. Verification means provider-reported published/ready state, a publication ID, and a canonical link; public-browser access is not required.

## Partial publication

A job with successful publications on fewer than all required platforms. It is not Completed; known successful links remain recorded.

## Pipeline pause

A persistent stop on further task processing after a failure, awaiting user action.

## Narration

The spoken content of a video, generated from the task or supplied directly. It uses one English voice identity and targets roughly 60 seconds.

## Presenter

A visible talking person in a video. Presenters are excluded from the requested future product.

## Incident recovery

User action following a terminal failure. It is distinct from the fully automated healthy content pipeline.
